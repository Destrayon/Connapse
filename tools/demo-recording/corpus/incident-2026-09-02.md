# Incident review: slow checkouts during the September sale (2026-09-02)

**Impact:** 47 minutes of checkout latency above 3 seconds; 212 shoppers abandoned checkout.

## What happened

At 14:05 the sale started and checkout traffic tripled. The ledger database's autovacuum ran on the
`checkout_sessions` table and held locks that delayed charge inserts by up to 2 seconds. The charge
worker's retries (200 ms, 400 ms, 800 ms) added to the queue instead of draining it.

## What we changed

1. Raised the charge worker's queue concurrency from 4 to 8 for sale days.
2. Proposed moving checkout sessions out of the ledger database (became ADR-007).
3. Added an alert on ledger lock wait time above 500 ms.
