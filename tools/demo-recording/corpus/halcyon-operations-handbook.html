Halcyon operations handbook

On-call guide for the Halcyon payments service. Version 3, October 2026.

1. When the card processor times out

The charge worker retries a timed-out processor call three times, waiting 200 ms, 400 ms and 800 ms. After the third failure it marks the charge needs_review and moves on. Do not replay needs_review charges by hand: the processor may have taken the money. Open the processor dashboard, match the charge by its idempotency key, and settle or refund it there.

2. When the ledger fails over

Patroni promotes the synchronous replica within 30 seconds. The charge worker pauses while the ledger is read-only and resumes on its own. Check that the replication lag panel returns to zero, then open a ticket to rebuild the old primary as the new replica.

3. Refunds

A refund is a new ledger row that points at the original charge; the charge row is never edited. Partial refunds are allowed up to the charged amount. Refunds older than 180 days go through the processor's dispute flow instead.

4. Sale days

Raise the charge worker's queue concurrency to 8 the morning of a sale and set it back to 4 the next day. Watch the ledger lock-wait alert (500 ms).
