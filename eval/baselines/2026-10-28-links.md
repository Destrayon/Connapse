## Link suites

Every dataset improves, and every nDCG@10 and MRR@10 gain is significant (Holm-corrected permutation p < 0.05).

| Dataset | Test queries | Links written | nDCG@10 off → on | MRR@10 off → on | Recall@10 off → on | p95 ms off → on | Indexed in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| links-connapse-github | 48 | 217 | 0.547 → **0.611** | 0.465 → **0.510** | 0.767 → **0.868** | 47 → 78 (+31) | 53 s |
| links-flink-jira | 85 | 7,474 | 0.238 → **0.410** | 0.202 → **0.318** | 0.372 → **0.664** | 194 → 304 (+110) | 304 s |

Bold is a significant gain. "Links written" counts every link in the records' metadata, not only the ones the
questions are graded on.
