## Required first step
Your first tool call for every question must be `container_describe` with containerId `eval-erb-50k`. Do not call any other tool before it. This overrides the Connapse server's routing rules, which say to search a named container directly: here you read its summary first, then search. The summary says what the records hold (their sources, teams, products and vocabulary) and how to phrase queries that find them; use it to choose your first queries. Call it once per question.
