# erb-full

The whole EnterpriseRAG-Bench corpus (511,958 documents; four duplicated IDs keep their first copy), with the same 470 scored questions as [erb-50k](../erb-50k/card.md), rewritten in 10,000-row groups by `eval/tools/build_enterprise_suite.py`. Use it to check that an erb-50k result holds at full scale; indexing it takes hours.
